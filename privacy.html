Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how the mobile app Vampire Villager: Werewolf (Turkish: Vampir Köylü: Kurt Adam Oyunu; Android package com.mehmett1.vampirkoylu; referred to below as "the app") handles information. It also covers this website. On some devices the app's icon label may show a shorter or different name, but this policy applies to that same app.

In short: the app has no ads, no analytics SDKs, no accounts and no servers of our own. Player names, photos and settings stay on your device and are never sent to us. The only data the app sends is what RevenueCat needs to check, sell and restore the optional Premium purchase: a random app user ID, basic device and app information and, if you buy, your purchase details. This happens when the app starts, even if you never buy anything. Payment itself is handled by Google Play.

1. Who we are

The app is developed and published by Mehmet Sancaktutan, an individual developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the limited personal data described in this policy, as the term is used in the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and in Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Contact: desteksancaktutan@gmail.com

2. Data stored only on your device

To run the game, the app saves the following locally on your device, in the app's private storage (preferences and app files):

  • player names you type in, and any optional player photo you add;
  • your role setup, game settings (for example the discussion timer, narrator mode and sound volume) and chosen language;
  • a count of completed games and the date of the last rating prompt, so the app does not ask for a rating too often;
  • whether Premium is unlocked, and the RevenueCat software's cache (its random user ID and your Premium status), so the app also works offline. The ID is also sent to RevenueCat, as described in section 4.

This data is never sent to us, and we cannot access it. It stays on your device until you delete it (see Deleting your data). If you have turned on device backups (Android/Google backup or iCloud), your operating system may include the app's data, including player names and photos, in your own backup stored in your Google or Apple account. We cannot access these backups, and they are managed by your device settings. The app's fonts are bundled inside the app, so displaying text does not make any network requests.

3. Camera and photos

Adding a photo for a player is optional. When you choose to add one, the app opens your device's camera or photo picker through the operating system. The picture you take or select is copied into the app's private storage and used only as that player's in-game avatar. The app does not access the camera or your photo library at any other time, and it never uploads photos. On Android, the app does not ask for camera or photo permissions: it uses the system camera app and the system photo picker. On iOS, the system asks for your permission before the camera is used, and the photo picker lets you choose single photos without giving the app access to your whole library. You can refuse or later withdraw camera permission in your device settings, and the game still works without photos.

4. Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

The app is free. You can optionally buy Premium, a one-time purchase with no subscription.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play (and by Apple, should an iOS version be released). We never see or store your card or other payment details. Google shares limited transaction information with us, such as an order ID, the product, price, date and country, so that we can deal with refunds and support requests.
  • Purchase validation. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to confirm purchases and keep track of whether Premium is active. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore Premium, the RevenueCat software in the app communicates with RevenueCat's servers. RevenueCat receives:
    • a random app user ID generated by the RevenueCat software. It does not contain your name, email or any account, because the app has no accounts, but it is still treated as personal data because it is linked to your purchases;
    • purchase data: the product, price and currency, the store purchase token or transaction ID, and purchase and expiry timestamps;
    • basic device and app information: operating system and version, app version, and device locale/country;
    • your IP address, which is visible to RevenueCat as part of any normal internet request.
  • Purpose: to deliver the Premium content you paid for, to restore it on your other devices through your store account, and to prevent purchase fraud. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. We do not use it for advertising or profiling. RevenueCat also gives us aggregated purchase statistics (for example, the number of Premium purchases and revenue by country). We use them only to understand how the app is doing, never for advertising, and we do not combine them with any other data.

Providing this data is necessary to buy and restore Premium. Without it the purchase cannot be completed, but the free game works fully.

If you never make a purchase, the app still sends the random app user ID and the basic device and app information above to RevenueCat when it checks your Premium status and loads the store products.

5. Sharing, ratings and links

  • Share button: opens your operating system's share sheet with a short text and the store link. After a game this can include the result, such as the winning player's name. You choose whether and where to send it, and we receive nothing.
  • Rating prompt: uses the operating system's official in-app review feature (Google Play or the App Store). Any review you leave is handled by the store under its own policy.
  • Links (such as this privacy policy or the store page) open in your browser or the store app, and their own privacy policies apply there.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising ID. The app removes the Android advertising ID permission.
  • No analytics SDKs (such as Firebase or Google Analytics), crash-reporting or tracking software. The only statistics we see are the aggregated purchase figures described in section 4.
  • No accounts, sign-in or social login.
  • No servers or databases run by us. Your game data is never sent to us.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or other sensitive permissions.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

8. Legal bases

Personal data is collected electronically and automatically through the RevenueCat software in the app and through Google Play, and through the emails you send us.

  • Purchase data (RevenueCat, Google Play): needed to perform our contract with you, that is, to deliver and restore the Premium you bought (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)). Some of it is also processed in our legitimate interest in preventing fraud (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)), and to meet legal obligations such as tax and accounting rules (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).
  • Premium status checks and loading the store (RevenueCat, including for people who never buy): our legitimate interest in showing the store and unlocking Premium for people who have bought it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Support emails: our legitimate interest in answering your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)), or the performance of our contract with you when the request is about a purchase (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Data stored on your device is kept under your control and never reaches us.

9. Retention

  • On-device data stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Settings › Reset Data only clears the current player list and role setup (see section 10).
  • RevenueCat purchase records are kept for as long as they are needed to provide and restore your Premium and to meet legal obligations, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Google Play transaction records are kept by Google under its own policies and legal obligations.
  • Google Play order and earnings reports that we keep for tax purposes are kept for the period required by Turkish tax law (generally 5 years, Tax Procedure Law No. 213, Art. 253).
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, and are then deleted unless the law requires us to keep them.

10. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Reset Data clears the current player list and role setup. To permanently delete all of the app's data, including player photos, uninstall the app or clear its storage (below).
  • Everything on the device: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings (Android: Settings › Apps › the app › Storage › Clear storage). This removes all of the app's local data, including player photos.
  • RevenueCat records: email desteksancaktutan@gmail.com and ask for your purchase data to be deleted. The app has no accounts, so to help us find your records please include your Google Play order ID (it begins with "GPA." and appears in your purchase receipt email). We will delete the records at RevenueCat within 30 days. Your purchase itself stays in your store account, so you can still restore Premium later with Restore Purchases. If the app stays installed, it will contact RevenueCat again the next time it starts, and restoring Premium sends your purchase to RevenueCat again. To avoid this, uninstall the app before asking us to delete your records.

11. International transfers

RevenueCat and GitHub are based in the United States, and Google may process data in several countries, so personal data is transferred outside Türkiye, the EU/EEA and the UK. For GDPR purposes, we rely on the data processing terms and the safeguards recognised by law that these providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

12. Security

Data stored by the app lives in its private storage, which your operating system protects from other apps. All connections to Google Play and RevenueCat are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). We keep the amount of data involved to a minimum, and no data reaches servers of ours at all. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

13. Children

The app is a party game for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The app does not ask for anyone's identity, and names and photos typed in for a game stay on the device. If you believe a child has sent us personal data (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to your personal data, and to have it corrected, deleted, restricted or given to you in a portable format. You may also object to processing or withdraw consent. Under KVKK Article 11 you can also ask whether your data is processed, what it is used for, who it is shared with, and compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. Under KVKK Article 11 you can also ask for corrections or deletions to be notified to third parties the data was transferred to, and object to a result against you that arises only from automated analysis. To use any of these rights, email desteksancaktutan@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu), after first applying to us as required by KVKK Articles 13–14; in the EU/EEA or the UK, the authority in your country.

Because we do not hold your game data and the RevenueCat ID does not show who you are, we may need a purchase order ID to find records that relate to you.

15. This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party fonts and scripts. GitHub, as the host, may record technical information such as your IP address in its server logs to keep the service running and secure. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Email links open your own mail app, and anything you send is handled as described under support emails above.

16. Changes to this policy

If the app or this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change is significant, for example new types of data or new third parties, we will also mention it in the app's store listing or release notes before it takes effect.

Earlier versions: versions 1.0.6 and earlier had no in-app purchases and did not use RevenueCat. They downloaded their fonts from Google Fonts the first time they were needed, which exposed your IP address to Google. Please update to the latest version.
