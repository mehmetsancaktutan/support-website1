How it works

It's the classic Werewolf / Mafia game, except the phone does the moderator's job.

  1. Add players

    Type in 3 to 15 names. A photo for each player is optional.

  2. Pick roles

    Choose roles yourself or tap a quick preset.

  3. Pass the phone

    Each player looks at their secret role in private, then hands the phone on.

  4. Night falls

    The app calls each role in turn and works out who was attacked, saved or investigated.

  5. Day breaks

    Talk it over against the discussion timer, then vote someone out. Keep going until one side wins.

FAQ

Is it an online game?

No. Everyone plays in the same room on one phone that gets passed around. You don't need an internet connection to play. The app only uses the internet to check, buy or restore Premium.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Player names and photos are stored on your phone and never sent to us.

Are there ads?

None at all. The app also has no analytics or tracking tools.

I bought Premium. How do I restore it?

Sign in to your phone with the Google account you used to buy Premium, open the app's Shop screen and tap Restore Purchases. The purchase is linked to your store account, so it also works after you reinstall the app or move to a new phone.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. You pay once and there are no recurring charges. Google Play handles payment and refunds.

How many people can play?

From 3 players. The free version goes up to 8 players and Premium goes up to 15.

How do I delete my data?

All game data stays on your phone. Settings › Reset Data in the app permanently deletes saved players and their photos, the role setup and game settings (version 1.0.8 and later). Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything. The privacy policy has more detail.
